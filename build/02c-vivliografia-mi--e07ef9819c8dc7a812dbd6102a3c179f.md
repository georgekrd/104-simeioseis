## Βιβλία αναφοράς

  Strategies*. New York: IEEE Press, 2000.
- C. A. Floudas, *Nonlinear and Mixed-Integer Optimization: Fundamentals and Applications*.
  New York: Oxford University Press, 1995.
- J. J. Grainger and W. D. Stevenson, Jr., *Power System Analysis*. New York: McGraw-Hill,

- N. G. Hingorani and L. Gyugyi, *Understanding FACTS: Concepts and Technology of
  Flexible AC Transmission Systems*. New York: IEEE Press, 2000.
- P. Kundur, *Power System Stability and Control*. New York: McGraw-Hill, 1994.
- J. Machowski, Z. Lubosny, J. W. Bialek and J. R. Bumby, *Power System Dynamics:

- T. Van Cutsem and C. Vournas, *Voltage Stability of Electric Power Systems*. Boston, MA:
  Kluwer Academic Publishers, 1998.
